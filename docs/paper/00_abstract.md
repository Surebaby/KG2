# KG-ProWeight: Knowledge Graph-Anchored Process Rewards for Multi-Hop Retrieval-Augmented Generation

---

## Abstract

检索增强生成(RAG)使大语言模型(LLMs)能够将多跳推理锚定于外部证据。现有方法要么通过仅评估最终答案质量的结果奖励信号训练大语言模型，要么采用来自冻结文本评分模型的逐步骤过程奖励提供更密集的监督。两种范式均未提供显式的、机器可验证的机制来验证中间推理步骤的事实正确性。我们提出 KG-ProWeight，一个三阶段训练框架，在强化学习中引入外部知识图谱(Wikidata)作为逐步骤的事实性锚点。第一阶段通过对推理轨迹进行 Wikidata 拓扑验证自动构建过程奖励标签，免除人工标注；标签为 $[-1,1]$ 上的连续值（$\text{precision}\times\text{relevance}$），而非三值离散标签。第二阶段训练可学习的 α-门控，每一步动态加权 KG 衍生与文本衍生的奖励信号，在 KG 覆盖稀疏时自适应过渡至文本监督。第三阶段在此自适应复合奖励下施加强化学习。在 HotpotQA、2WikiMultiHopQA 和 MuSiQue 的全量 Wikipedia 检索 setting 下，KG-ProWeight 的 SFT+KG 变体在三个数据集上均高于纯文本 SFT 基线（HotpotQA +1.3pp, 2WikiMultiHopQA +2.7pp, MuSiQue +2.3pp），方向一致；但逐题配对检验（精确 McNemar / 配对 bootstrap）显示**没有一项达到统计显著**（p 从 0.091 到 0.618），故我们报告为方向一致的正向趋势而非已证实的提升 —— 把 n 从 300 提到 1000 只使 p 从 0.450 降到 0.091，说明受限的是**效应量**而非证据量。在单一固定状态下重测 α-门控（每数据集 n=300，同一实体链接缓存快照）后，我们**未**观察到跨数据集自适应：α=0.918 (HotpotQA) / 0.914 (2WikiMultiHopQA) / 0.908 (MuSiQue)，跨数据集极差 0.010，仅为单次运行内问题间标准差（最大 0.030）的三分之一；门控与子图密度的相关系数达 +0.90，而其余两个特征（链接置信度、语义熵）几乎不起作用。换言之，门控实际退化为单特征密度函数，而非按推理复杂度调节 KG 依赖——这是一个可复现的负面结论，也指出了自适应门控的具体改进方向。在我们训练过的任何配置下 PPO 变体均未超越 SFT，并且我们明确撤回早前「KG 分支是 RL 训练必要正则化器」的主张：在同一 checkpoint（而非混用）下读数，PPO+KG 在三个数据集上全线低于纯文本 SFT 基线，且 KG 通道实测奖励占比仅 2.7%。对 KG 管道中噪声来源的系统审计表明，约 70% 的原始三元组在充分过滤前不具信息量，将 KG 质量确立为下游性能的一阶决定因素。

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) enables large language models (LLMs) to ground multi-hop reasoning in external evidence. Existing methods either train LLMs through outcome-based reward signals that evaluate only the final answer quality, or employ per-step process rewards from frozen text-scoring models to provide denser supervision. Neither paradigm provides an explicit, machine-verifiable mechanism to verify the factual correctness of intermediate reasoning steps. We propose KG-ProWeight, a three-phase training framework that introduces an external knowledge graph (Wikidata) as a per-step factuality anchor in reinforcement learning. Phase 1 auto-constructs process-reward labels through topological verification of reasoning trajectories against Wikidata, eliminating manual annotation; the label is continuous on $[-1,1]$ ($\text{precision}\times\text{relevance}$) rather than three-valued. Phase 2 trains a learned α-gate that dynamically weights KG-derived and text-derived reward signals per step, enabling adaptive transition to text-based signals where KG coverage is sparse. Phase 3 applies reinforcement learning under this adaptive composite reward. On HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and MuSiQue under full Wikipedia retrieval, the SFT+KG variant of KG-ProWeight scores above the text-only SFT baseline on all three datasets (HotpotQA +1.3pp, 2WikiMultiHopQA +2.7pp, MuSiQue +2.3pp), consistent in direction—but under per-question paired testing (exact McNemar / paired bootstrap) **none of these gains reaches significance** (p from 0.091 to 0.618), so we report a consistent positive direction rather than a demonstrated improvement; raising n from 300 to 1000 moves p only from 0.450 to 0.091, indicating the binding constraint is effect size, not evidence. Re-measuring the α-gate under a single fixed state (n=300 per dataset, one frozen entity-linking cache snapshot), we find **no** cross-dataset adaptation: α=0.918 on HotpotQA, 0.914 on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and 0.908 on MuSiQue—a spread of 0.010, one third of the largest within-run standard deviation across questions (0.030). The gate correlates with subgraph density at r≈+0.90 while its other two features (link confidence, semantic entropy) contribute almost nothing, so it has collapsed into a single-feature density function rather than modulating KG reliance by reasoning complexity. We report this as a reproducible negative result and identify what an adaptive gate would need in order to express real variation. The PPO variants do not surpass SFT under any configuration we trained, and we explicitly withdraw an earlier claim that the KG branch acts as a necessary regularizer for RL: read off a single checkpoint rather than a mixture, PPO+KG falls below the text-only SFT baseline on all three datasets, and the KG channel's measured reward share is only 2.7%. A systematic audit of KG pipeline noise reveals that ~70% of raw triples are uninformative before filtering, establishing KG quality as a first-order determinant of downstream performance.
