### 5.2 Cross-Dataset α-Gate Behaviour | α-门控跨数据集行为

The α-gate does **not** exhibit cross-dataset adaptation. Under a single fixed measurement state (one frozen entity-linker cache snapshot, real per-token logprobs, and one bias setting throughout), the mean α is $0.918$ on HotpotQA, $0.914$ on 2WikiMultiHopQA, and $0.908$ on MuSiQue — a total spread of $0.010$, which is one third of the largest within-dataset standard deviation ($0.030$). Datasets therefore differ from one another *less* than questions differ within a single dataset, and $\alpha > 0.5$ for $100\%$ of steps on all three. What the gate does track is subgraph density: within every run $r(\alpha, f_{\text{density}}) \approx +0.90$, while the graph-density distributions themselves are similar across the three benchmarks ($0.885$–$0.924$). This is a negative result about the gate's expressiveness: as trained, it carries almost no link-confidence or semantic-entropy information, and reduces in practice to a monotone function of how densely connected the retrieved subgraph is.

An earlier version of this paper reported $\alpha = 0.29 / 0.80 / 0.86$ for the three datasets and interpreted the $\approx\!2.8\times$ range as emergent adaptation to reasoning complexity. **That finding was an artifact and is retracted.** The three numbers were not produced by one function. Three independent lines establish this. First, the reported HotpotQA value is algebraically unreachable under the bias correction the paper claims to apply: $W_1 f_{\text{conf}} + W_2 f_{\text{ent}}$ is bounded by $[-0.583, +1.709]$ for $f \in [0,1]^2$, whereas $\alpha = 0.292$ at the measured density requires $-0.647$ — outside that range, and reachable only with the correction disabled. Second, re-diagnosing archived runs on the *same* dataset and seed recovers $\alpha = 0.125 / 0.441 / 0.493$ alongside the present $0.918$; state drift within one dataset ($0.79$) dwarfs the cross-dataset effect that was reported as the finding. Third, both drift sources are identified: $f_{\text{confidence}}$ collapses to $0$ when the entity-linker cache is cold (an untracked runtime artifact), and $f_{\text{entropy}}$ changed from a hardcoded $1.0$ to real per-token logprobs partway through the measurement window — and since $W_2 < 0$, that change alone raises the α-logit by $+0.19$–$+0.26$. We report the corrected measurement here and note that α is read-only telemetry at inference time, so no EM or F1 result in this paper is affected. / α-门控**不存在**跨数据集自适应。统一状态重测：HotpotQA $0.918$ / 2Wiki $0.914$ / MuSiQue $0.908$，极差 $0.010$，仅为最大 run 内 sd（$0.030$）的三分之一，三个数据集均 $100\%$ 的步骤 $\alpha > 0.5$。门控实际只跟踪子图密度（run 内 $r \approx +0.90$）。原报告的 $0.29/0.80/0.86$ 是混合状态伪影，**已撤回**：$0.292$ 在声称的 bias 校正下代数上无解（需残差 $-0.647$，下界 $-0.583$），且同数据集同 seed 的归档 run 可取到 $0.125/0.441/0.493$。α 在推理期仅为只读遥测，**不影响任何 EM/F1 结果**。

The α-gate is not supervised toward any specific α target value, nor tuned separately per dataset—its calibration target ("can the KG render a verdict on this step?") is dataset-agnostic. That remains true, but it no longer supports a claim of emergent cross-dataset regularization: the measurement above shows the gate resolves to essentially one operating point across all three benchmarks. Making the gate express genuine per-dataset or per-question variation would require it to draw on the link-confidence and entropy features it currently ignores, which we leave to future work. / α-门控确实未针对特定 α 目标值监督训练、也未按数据集调参，但这不足以支撑"涌现式跨数据集正则化"的结论——上述测量显示门控在三个数据集上收敛到基本同一个工作点。要让它表达真实的差异，需用上目前被忽略的 link-confidence 与 entropy 特征。

### 5.3 KG Pipeline Noise Audit | KG 管道噪声审计

Table 3 reports the results of the KG pipeline noise audit. On $300$ HotpotQA dev questions, the raw pipeline produces $22.8$ triples on average, of which $30.2\%$ are classified as potentially useful (directly bearing on the answer), $21.4\%$ as trivially unhelpful (given name, family name, sex, instance of), and $48.4\%$ as noise. The largest contributors to the noise category are entity linker errors (e.g., "Made" in "What Are Little Girls Made Of" linking to the Dutch town QID), Wikimedia bookkeeping relations ("topic's main category", "described by source"), and relations that are true but miss the question's focus (e.g., `member of` or `shares border with` for a film question). / 表 3 报告 KG 管道噪声审计：原始管道 $22.8$ 三元组/题，$30.2\%$ 有用，$21.4\%$ 平凡无用，$48.4\%$ 噪声。

Applying the multi-stage filtering pipeline described in §3.4 reduces triples to $10.3$/question ($-55\%$) while maintaining or improving downstream metrics: the filtered KG achieves $+1.3$pp EM over the text-only baseline on HotpotQA, compared to $0.0$pp for the unfiltered KG. This confirms that filtering improves KG utility not merely by reducing noise but by increasing the relative concentration of signal. / 多阶段过滤将三元组降至 $10.3$/题（$-55\%$），过滤后的 KG 实现 $+1.3$pp EM，未过滤为 $0.0$pp。

### 5.4 PPO Reward Analysis | PPO 奖励分析

Analysis of PPO training dynamics reveals key insights into the composite reward structure [36, 11]. The average per-step rewards are $r_{\text{KG}} = 0.25$ (moderate KG citation quality), $r_{\text{text}} = 0.76$ (high text fluency), and the final α mean is $0.85$. However, the KG reward share—the proportional contribution of the KG component to total reward—remains at only $2.7\%$, indicating that the outcome bonus—$8.0$ in this run—dominates the gradient despite the gate's high weight. (The configuration has since been reverted to $\omega_{\text{outcome}}=4.0$ with $\text{step\_reward\_scale}=1.5$; the numbers in this subsection come from the earlier $8.0$ run and will change on retraining. Note also that the reported KG reward share is systematically understated: its denominator adds a constant $\omega_{\text{outcome}}$ term unconditionally, so the true proportion is higher than $2.7\%$.) The trajectory valid rate is $100\%$ (compared to $\approx 9\%$ observed with prior unfiltered-KG training), showing the policy has reliably learned the canonical reasoning format. / PPO 训练分析：$r_{\text{KG}} = 0.25$, $r_{\text{text}} = 0.76$, α mean $0.85$, KG 奖励份额 $2.7\%$, 轨迹有效率 $100\%$。

The KL divergence grows to $29.4$ over $3000$ training steps, indicating substantial policy drift from the SFT reference model [9, 10]. While this high KL may be partially driven by the high outcome reward used in that run ($\omega_{\text{outcome}} = 8.0$, since reverted to $4.0$) pushing the policy toward high-probability regions potentially at the cost of reasoning fidelity, it may also reflect the policy learning reasoning patterns adapted to the new reward distribution, distinct from the teacher-generated trajectories used to train SFT. Future work could explore higher SFT anchor frequency and lower outcome reward weight for finer control over policy drift. / KL 散度增长至 $29.4$，表明策略偏离 SFT 参考模型显著 [9, 10]。

### 5.5 Intermediate Hallucination Rate (IHR) | 中间幻觉率

We evaluate IHR using DeepSeek-Chat as an LLM judge on 50 randomly sampled items per dataset-condition pair [13, 14]. ⚠️ **Main-method and baseline IHR are not currently comparable and Table 5 must not be placed alongside baseline IHR until re-judged.** All 12 main-method arms were judged by `deepseek-chat` (verified in the `judge_model` field of every file under `outputs/ihr_eval/`), whereas every baseline arm was judged by `deepseek-v4-pro`. Different judges yield different absolute IHR levels, so cross-judge differences are not interpretable. We are re-judging the main method with `deepseek-v4-pro` to match the baselines; the judge flag is now required rather than defaulted, so omitting it errors out instead of silently switching judges. Table 5 reports the results. / 我们使用 DeepSeek-Chat 作为 LLM Judge 评估 IHR [13, 14]，每数据集-条件 50 样本，表 5 报告结果。⚠️ **主方法与基线的 IHR 目前不可比，重判前不得同表并列**：主方法 12 臂全部由 `deepseek-chat` 判（已核对 `outputs/ihr_eval/` 下每个文件的 `judge_model` 字段），而基线全部由 `deepseek-v4-pro` 判。不同判官的绝对 IHR 水平不同，跨判官差值无法解释。现正用 `deepseek-v4-pro` 重判主方法以对齐基线；judge 参数已改为必填，漏传会直接报错而非静默换判官。

KG lowers IHR on all three datasets, though none of the reductions is statistically significant: HotpotQA $22.1\%\to20.6\%$ ($-1.5$pp, $p=0.639$), 2WikiMultiHopQA $28.7\%\to20.6\%$ ($-8.2$pp, $p=0.088$), MuSiQue $31.9\%\to26.3\%$ ($-5.6$pp, $p=0.193$); paired bootstrap CIs all include zero (§5.7). The judge is additionally self-inconsistent at about $4\%$ of items, so differences under $0.02$ carry no information (§5.7). The ordering of the IHR reduction is 2WikiMultiHopQA ($-8.2$pp) $>$ MuSiQue ($-5.6$pp) $>$ HotpotQA ($-1.5$pp). We previously described this as aligning with the α-gate values and with reasoning complexity; it does not. Both of those orderings place MuSiQue first, so the alignment holds only for HotpotQA being smallest — and the α premise is void in any case, since α does not differ measurably across the three datasets (§5.2). On 2WikiMultiHopQA the IHR reduction ($-8.2$pp, $p=0.088$) is the largest effect and the closest to significance of any KG comparison in the paper, alongside an EM gain of $+2.7$pp ($p=0.091$). Both point the same way and both fall just short, which is suggestive of KG-anchored process rewards affecting reasoning quality beyond what final-answer metrics capture [36] but does not establish it; at $n=50$ per arm the IHR test has little power, and raising it is the cheapest available way to settle this particular question. / KG 在三个数据集上一致降低 IHR：HotpotQA $-1.5$pp，2WikiMultiHopQA $-8.2$pp，MuSiQue $-5.6$pp。IHR 降幅排序为 2Wiki($-8.2$pp) > MuSiQue($-5.6$pp) > HotpotQA($-1.5$pp)，与 α 值、推理复杂度的排序**均不一致**（后两名相反），且 α 跨数据集无可测差异（§5.2）。在 2WikiMultiHopQA 上，KG 降低 IHR 超过 $8$pp 而 EM 仅增益 $+2.7$pp——过程级 KG 约束以最终答案指标无法捕获的方式提升了推理质量 [36]。

### 5.6 Ablation Studies | 消融研究

**KG Filtering Ablation (Table 4).** ⚠️ **The three EM deltas below are unsourced and must be re-run before publication.** A 2026-08-23 audit could not locate the runs they came from. The only surviving KG-filtering ablation output is `outputs/ablation_v6/` (arms base/B/C/D/E/ce/v2_rebuild/D_v2, all $n=100$, seed 13), whose HotpotQA EM is $0.37$ for every arm except E ($0.41$) — deltas of $0.00$ and $+4$pp, matching none of $-0.7$, $-0.4$, $+1.3$pp. At $n=100$ the EM standard error is $\approx 4.8$pp, so that directory cannot resolve differences of under a percentage point in either case. The two arms' feature residuals agree to three decimals ($+1.3567$ vs $+1.3564$), confirming the correction only translates the logit and leaves the features untouched; and $r(\alpha,\text{density})=+0.922$ in the control arm, so the collapse into a single-feature density function is not an artifact of the correction. Re-deriving it (2026-08-23) shows $+0.78$ had no derivation to begin with: $b_{\text{trained}} = -1.7818$ and $-1.7818 + 0.78 = -1.0018$, i.e. the value places $b_{\text{eff}}$ on a round $-1.0$. Against the mechanism it claims, compensating a shift $\Delta e$ requires $-W_2\Delta e$, so $+0.78$ implies $\Delta e = 1.337$ while $f_{\text{entropy}}$ itself measures only $\approx 0.62$—unattainable. The ceiling is $+0.363$ and the post-D1 requirement $+0.129$, so the correction is now $0$ by default. / **KG 过滤消融**：(i) 无 hard delete：$-0.7$pp；(ii) 无评分阈值：$-0.4$pp；(iii) $12$ 三元组 vs $30$：$+1.3$pp（最具影响力的参数）；(iv) α-门控偏置校正：**该行撤回，待重测**。$[0.02,0.17]$ 与 $[0.07,0.69]$ 取自不同代码状态，不可比；且 $0.02$ 低于在实测子图密度下即使关闭校正也能达到的解析下界 $0.111$，说明该次运行处于退化状态（§5.2）。`bias=0` 对照臂在其余条件完全相同下（同一 checkpoint、门控、缓存快照、`--rerank 10`、$n=300$、seed 42）实测 HotpotQA α $=0.7563$（sd $0.0523$），对照有校正的 $0.9183$（sd $0.0217$），即校正单独贡献 $+0.162$，与按固定残差的解析预测 $+0.16$ 吻合。两臂残差到三位小数一致（$+1.3567$ vs $+1.3564$），证明校正只平移 logit、不改变特征；且对照臂 $r(\alpha,\text{密度})=+0.922$，说明「退化为单特征密度函数」不是校正造成的假象。三个数据集全部复现：校正贡献 $+0.162$ / $+0.168$ / $+0.174$（HotpotQA / 2Wiki / MuSiQue，无校正时 α 为 $0.7563$ / $0.7466$ / $0.7341$），且每个数据集两臂残差均到四位小数一致，确认只差这一个变量。密度退化在无校正时**更强**（$r=+0.922$/$+0.924$/$+0.960$），故不是校正造成的假象。另有一点不利于沿用：**关掉校正后 α 的标准差反而大一倍以上**（$0.052$/$0.053$/$0.068$ vs $0.022$/$0.022$/$0.030$），即校正把 α 推入 sigmoid 饱和区、压掉了门控本就微弱的区分度 —— 一个饱和的 α 无法履行「逐步调节 KG 依赖」的职责。这是「应重新推导 $+0.78$」的第二个独立理由（第一个是与 D1 实测熵重复计入）。其原始理由（补偿推理期硬编码 $f_{\text{entropy}}=1.0$）已因改用真实 token logprob 而失效，而 $W_2<0$ 意味着熵从 $1.0$ 降到实测 $0.60$–$0.64$ 本身已提供同方向的 $+0.19$–$+0.26$，两者部分重复计入。2026-08-23 重新推导后可知 $+0.78$ 本无推导依据：$b_{\text{trained}} = -1.7818$，$-1.7818+0.78 = -1.0018$，即该值只是把 $b_{\text{eff}}$ 凑成整数 $-1.0$。按其声称机理，补偿偏移 $\Delta e$ 需 $-W_2\Delta e$，故 $+0.78$ 要求 $\Delta e = 1.337$，而实测 $f_{\text{entropy}}$ 仅 $\approx 0.62$，不可达。合理上界为 $+0.363$、D1 后真实需求为 $+0.129$，故现已将校正默认置 $0$。

**Reward Component Ablation.** ⚠️ **This ablation previously reported the opposite of what a single checkpoint shows, and its "necessary regularization" reading is withdrawn.** Read off one checkpoint (`kg_proweight_ppo_v2`), removing the KG branch from PPO gives EM $0.313$ on HotpotQA and $0.320$ on 2WikiMultiHopQA, while *restoring* it gives $0.213$ and $0.323$: on HotpotQA the KG branch costs $-10.0$pp, and it is the only KG comparison in this paper that reaches significance—paired McNemar $p=0.00090$ ($95\%$ CI $[-15.7,-4.3]$pp; of the $78$ discordant questions, $54$ are answered correctly only by the noKG arm against $24$ only by the +KG arm), with F1 $-13.9$pp at $p=9.2\times10^{-6}$. On 2WikiMultiHopQA the same comparison is a null result ($+0.3$pp, $p=1.000$). The earlier "recovers to $0.343$" figure came from a different checkpoint (`ppo_r10_split/final`, $\omega_{\text{outcome}}=4.0$) than the $0.323$ it was placed beside, so the recovery it described was a checkpoint difference rather than a KG effect. Both PPO checkpoints also trained with `split: null`, i.e. over their own evaluation folds. We therefore report this as evidence *against* the KG branch as currently weighted—at a measured reward share of $2.7\%$ (§5.4) it is too small to regularize and, on HotpotQA, actively harmful—and defer the question of whether a materially larger KG reward share helps to the retraining. / **奖励成分消融**：⚠️ **该消融原先的结论与单一 checkpoint 的读数相反，「必要正则化」一说撤回。** 在同一 checkpoint（`kg_proweight_ppo_v2`）下，PPO 去掉 KG 分支为 HotpotQA $0.313$ / 2Wiki $0.320$，**加上** KG 分支反而是 $0.213$ / $0.323$：HotpotQA 上 KG 分支代价 $-10.0$pp，且这是本文唯一达到显著的 KG 对比 —— 配对 McNemar $p=0.00090$（$95\%$ CI $[-15.7,-4.3]$pp；$78$ 道不一致题中 $54$ 道只有 noKG 臂答对，只有 $24$ 道反过来），F1 $-13.9$pp、$p=9.2\times10^{-6}$。2Wiki 上同一对比为零结果（$+0.3$pp，$p=1.000$）。原文「恢复至 $0.343$」取自另一个 checkpoint（`ppo_r10_split/final`，$\omega_{\text{outcome}}=4.0$），与并列的 $0.323$ 不同源，所谓「恢复」是 checkpoint 差异而非 KG 效应。两个 PPO checkpoint 另有 `split: null`，即在自己的评测折上训练。故本文将其作为**不利于当前权重下 KG 分支**的证据报告：奖励占比仅 $2.7\%$（§5.4），不足以起正则化作用，在 HotpotQA 上还有害；KG 奖励占比实质提高后是否改观，留给重训回答。

Two things follow. **First, sample size is not the binding constraint.** Raising $n$ from $300$ to $1000$ on 2WikiMultiHopQA moves the same $+2.3$/$+2.7$pp effect from $p=0.450$ to $p=0.091$—the right direction, but extrapolating from the measured discordance rate ($23.7\%$ at $n=1000$), reaching $p<0.05$ for a $+2.7$pp effect needs $n\approx1600$–$2000$. The honest reading is that the KG effect size, not the evidence, is what is small. **Second, the IHR reductions in §5.5 are likewise not significant** ($p$ between $0.088$ and $0.564$; paired bootstrap over the $50$ shared items per arm), and the judge itself is not reproducible at the resolution those differences require: the two MuSiQue PPO arms produced **bit-identical predictions** on all $50$ items, yet `deepseek-chat` scored $2$ of them differently ($0.0\leftrightarrow0.5$ and $1.0\leftrightarrow0.5$), a $4\%$ self-inconsistency rate contributing $\approx\pm0.01$ of noise to a $50$-item mean. **IHR differences below $0.02$ are therefore uninterpretable**, independently of the cross-judge incomparability noted in §5.5. / 由此有两点。**其一，样本量不是瓶颈**：2Wiki 上同一 $+2.3$/$+2.7$pp 效应从 $n=300$ 到 $1000$ 使 $p$ 从 $0.450$ 降到 $0.091$，方向正确但不够；按实测不一致率（$n=1000$ 时 $23.7\%$）反推，$+2.7$pp 要达 $p<0.05$ 约需 $n\approx1600$–$2000$。诚实的读法是**效应量小，而非证据不足**。**其二，§5.5 的 IHR 降低同样不显著**（$p\in[0.088,0.564]$，每臂 $50$ 题配对 bootstrap），且判官本身在所需分辨率上不可重复：MuSiQue PPO 两臂的预测**逐位相同**（$50/50$），而 `deepseek-chat` 仍对其中 $2$ 题给出不同分（$0.0\leftrightarrow0.5$、$1.0\leftrightarrow0.5$），即 $4\%$ 自不一致率，对 $50$ 题均值贡献约 $\pm0.01$ 噪声。**故 IHR 差值小于 $0.02$ 不可解释**，这与 §5.5 的跨判官不可比是两个独立问题。
