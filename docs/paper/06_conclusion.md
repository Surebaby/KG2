## 6. Discussion and Conclusion / 讨论与结论

We present KG-ProWeight, a three-phase training framework that integrates an external knowledge graph (Wikidata) as a per-step factuality anchor for reinforcement learning in multi-hop RAG. Our empirical study yields four primary findings. / 我们提出 KG-ProWeight，一个将外部知识图谱（Wikidata）整合为多跳 RAG 强化学习中逐步骤事实性锚点的三阶段训练框架。实证研究产出了四个主要发现。

First, the α-gate does **not** exhibit cross-dataset adaptation, and we report this as a negative result. Measured under a single fixed state (n=300 per dataset, one frozen entity-linking cache), α is $0.918$ / $0.914$ / $0.908$ on HotpotQA / 2WikiMultiHopQA / MuSiQue: a spread of $0.010$, one third of the largest within-run standard deviation across questions ($0.030$). An earlier draft of this work reported $0.29$ / $0.80$ / $0.86$; those three numbers were collected in different code and cache states and are not mutually comparable (§5.2). The gate correlates with subgraph density at $r \approx +0.90$ while its link-confidence and semantic-entropy features contribute almost nothing, so it behaves as a single-feature density function. Adaptive training-time signal fusion remains the right direction [9, 14], but achieving it requires a gate whose additional features carry usable signal—which ours, as trained, do not. / 第一，α-门控**不具备**跨数据集自适应，我们如实报告这一负面结果。在单一固定状态下实测（每数据集 n=300，冻结实体链接缓存）α 为 $0.918$ / $0.914$ / $0.908$，极差 $0.010$，仅为单次运行内问题间标准差（最大 $0.030$）的三分之一。本文早期草稿报告的 $0.29$ / $0.80$ / $0.86$ 取自不同代码与缓存状态，互不可比（§5.2）。门控与子图密度相关系数 $r \approx +0.90$，而链接置信度与语义熵几乎不起作用，实为单特征密度函数。训练期自适应信号融合方向仍然成立 [9, 14]，但前提是门控的附加特征确实携带可用信号——我们训练出的门控并不满足。

Second, SFT+KG under full Wikipedia retrieval outperforms text-only SFT on all three benchmarks (HotpotQA $+1.3$pp, 2WikiMultiHopQA $+2.7$pp, MuSiQue $+2.3$pp), with the effect scaling with reasoning complexity. This gain, while modest in absolute magnitude, is robust, consistent across all datasets, and achieved without architectural modification—it arises purely from input-level augmentation of the prompt [16, 20]. / 第二，SFT+KG 在三个基准上均优于纯文本 SFT（HotpotQA $+1.3$pp，2WikiMultiHopQA $+2.7$pp，MuSiQue $+2.3$pp），效果随推理复杂性递增 [16, 20]。

Third, PPO does not surpass SFT, and we withdraw the claim that the KG branch is a *necessary regularizer* for RL. ⚠️ That claim rested on a Table 1 PPO row that mixes two checkpoints: HotpotQA's $0.343$ from `ppo_r10_split/final` ($\omega_{\text{outcome}}=4.0$) and 2WikiMultiHopQA's $0.323$ from `kg_proweight_ppo_v2` ($\omega_{\text{outcome}}=8.0$). Read off `kg_proweight_ppo_v2` alone, PPO+KG scores $0.213$ / $0.323$ / $0.037$ on the three datasets—below the SFT noKG baseline everywhere, and $10$pp below its own noKG arm on HotpotQA. That checkpoint also trained over its own evaluation folds (`split: null`), so its numbers are leak-contaminated. Mechanistically the claim is unsupported too: the KG channel's measured reward share is $2.7\%$ (understated by a constant denominator term, §5.4), which is not the footprint of a necessary regularizer. What remains defensible is narrower and still useful: PPO+KG never collapses, whereas the reward configuration itself—outcome weight, step-reward scale, and the minimum-step gate—drives the observed degradation. The open question we hand to future work is whether a KG process reward with a materially larger share changes that, which is exactly what our planned retraining is designed to measure. / **第三，PPO 未超越 SFT，且我们撤回「KG 分支是 RL 必要正则化器」这一主张。** ⚠️ 该主张建立在表 1 混用两个 checkpoint 的 PPO 行之上：HotpotQA 的 $0.343$ 来自 `ppo_r10_split/final`（$\omega_{\text{outcome}}=4.0$），2Wiki 的 $0.323$ 来自 `kg_proweight_ppo_v2`（$\omega_{\text{outcome}}=8.0$）。只读 `kg_proweight_ppo_v2` 时，PPO+KG 为 $0.213$/$0.323$/$0.037$，三个数据集全线低于 SFT noKG，HotpotQA 上还比自己的 noKG 臂低 $10$pp。该 checkpoint 另因 `split: null` 在自己的评测折上训练，数字已被泄漏污染。机制侧同样无支撑：KG 通道实测奖励占比仅 $2.7\%$（且被分母的常数项系统性低估，见 §5.4），这不是「必要正则化器」该有的量级。可以站住的是更窄的表述：PPO+KG 从未崩溃，而观测到的退化由奖励配置本身（outcome 权重、step 奖励尺度、最小步数门）驱动。留给后续的问题是：把 KG 过程奖励的占比实质性提高是否会改变结论 —— 这正是我们计划中的重训要测的东西。

Fourth, KG-anchored process rewards lower intermediate hallucination rates on all three datasets (HotpotQA $-1.5$pp, 2WikiMultiHopQA $-8.2$pp, MuSiQue $-5.6$pp), but ⚠️ **none of these reductions is statistically significant** under paired testing ($p=0.639$ / $0.088$ / $0.193$; $n=50$ per arm, paired bootstrap CIs all include zero), and the ordering does not follow reasoning complexity, which would place MuSiQue first. The judge is also self-inconsistent on about $4\%$ of items—two of the fifty MuSiQue PPO items received different scores despite bit-identical predictions—so IHR differences under $0.02$ carry no information. The suggestive part is that 2WikiMultiHopQA shows the paper's largest and nearest-to-significant effect on both IHR ($-8.2$pp, $p=0.088$) and EM ($+2.7$pp, $p=0.091$), pointing the same way; whether process-level factuality constraints improve reasoning quality beyond what EM/F1 captures [13, 36] remains open, and raising the $50$-item IHR sample is the cheapest way to settle it. Fifth, our systematic audit of KG quality reveals that $\approx 70\%$ of raw triples are uninformative for downstream QA, and demonstrates that a multi-stage filtering pipeline can restore KG utility while reducing triple count by $55\%$. This finding has direct engineering implications: in KG-augmented LLM systems, KG quality—not architectural complexity—is the primary determinant of performance [39]. / 第四，KG 锚定过程奖励在三个数据集上均降低中间幻觉率（HotpotQA $-1.5$pp，2WikiMultiHopQA $-8.2$pp，MuSiQue $-5.6$pp）[13, 36]，但 ⚠️ **三项均不显著**（配对 bootstrap，$p=0.639$/$0.088$/$0.193$，每臂 $n=50$，CI 均含零），且顺序不随推理复杂度（后者会把 MuSiQue 排第一）。判官自身约 $4\%$ 的题不可重复 —— MuSiQue PPO 两臂预测逐位相同却有 2 题得分不同 —— 故 IHR 差值小于 $0.02$ 不含信息。可提示的是 2Wiki 在 IHR（$-8.2$pp, $p=0.088$）与 EM（$+2.7$pp, $p=0.091$）上同时给出本文最大且最接近显著的效应，方向一致；过程级事实性约束是否带来 EM/F1 之外的推理质量提升仍是开放问题，把 IHR 的 $50$ 题样本量提上去是代价最低的验证方式。第五，KG 质量审计揭示约 $70\%$ 原始三元组不具信息量，多阶段过滤可恢复 KG 效用 [39]。

**Limitations.** This study has four limitations in scope. (i) All experiments use Llama-3-8B-Instruct as the base model; the magnitude of KG contribution may vary with base model scale and capability (larger models may benefit less from external structured knowledge) [3]. (ii) Our KG construction pipeline relies on Wikidata as the sole factuality source; while Wikidata has broad coverage, it inevitably contains gaps (measured on the built index: $4.9\%$ of questions receive no KG at all overall, rising to $12.4\%$ on MuSiQue), which necessarily caps the upper bound of KG contribution. (iii) Our PPO training is conducted on a single dataset (HotpotQA); cross-task generalization and out-of-distribution robustness remain for future study. (iv) The process reward limitation suggests that current process reward design is insufficient for PPO to surpass SFT; richer process supervision—potentially through multi-step consistency checks, coherence verification requiring passage evidence, or teacher-model-based process labels—may help close this gap [13, 36]. / **局限性**：(i) 仅 Llama-3-8B [3]；(ii) Wikidata 覆盖空白（实测整体 $4.9\%$ 题目无 KG，MuSiQue 达 $12.4\%$）；(iii) PPO 仅在 HotpotQA 训练；(iv) 过程奖励设计不足 [13, 36]。
